The **Members** page (**Settings → Members**) manages who can access your organization. Members
are **organization-scoped**, not per-site: a member of the org can reach every site under it, so
the page lists everyone in the organization even though it opens from inside a site's settings.

## Roles

Every member has one of three roles:

- **Owner** — full control, including deleting the organization. The creator is the first owner.
- **Admin** — can manage sites, settings, and members.
- **Member** — can access the org's sites.

Only **owners and admins** can invite, re-role, or remove members.

## Changing a member's role

The **Role** column is editable in place: pick a new role and it applies immediately. What you
can grant depends on what you are — an **owner** can grant `member`, `admin`, or `owner`; an
**admin** can grant `member` or `admin` (only an owner can create another owner, and only an
owner can change an existing owner's role). You can't change your own role, and the last owner
can't be demoted.

Granting `owner` or `admin` is also how you make an organization an eligible **site-transfer
destination** — [transferring a site](/control-plane/danger-zone#transfer-a-site) requires
owner/admin on both ends.

## Inviting people

In **Invite member**, enter one or more email addresses separated by commas or spaces, pick the
**role** they'll join with (defaults to `member`; the same grant rules apply), then
**Send Invite**. Each address becomes a **pending invitation** carrying that role. Papervine
reports the result per address — newly invited, already a member, or already invited — so a
partly-duplicate paste still sends the new ones.

Each pending invitation has a **shareable accept link**. Use **Copy link** and send it to the
invitee however you like (chat, your own email). The link is the invitation: anyone who opens it
is taken to an **accept** page for your organization.

A pending invitation expires after a couple of days. You can **Cancel** it any time before it's
accepted, which immediately invalidates its link.

## Accepting an invitation

When someone opens an invite link:

- If they're **signed in to Papervine with the invited email**, they see the organization and an
  **Accept** button — accepting adds them as a member and drops them into the dashboard.
- If they **aren't signed in**, they're prompted to **sign up or log in with the invited email**
  first, then returned to the accept page. An account is required to join — the email they use
  must match the one the invitation was sent to.
- If they're signed in as a **different** account, Papervine asks them to switch to the invited
  email.

## Removing members

The trash control beside a member removes them from the organization (you can't remove yourself
this way). Removing a member revokes their access to every site in the org.
