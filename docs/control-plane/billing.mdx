title: "Plans & billing"
description: "How plans, the 30-day trial, and AI credits work"
---

Billing is scoped to the **organization**: one plan and one credit pool cover every
site the org owns. It lives under a site's **Settings**, in the Workspace section:

- **Settings → Billing** — the current plan, changing plans, and downgrading.
- **Settings → Usage** — the credit meter, the reset date, credit packs, and recent
  AI usage.

Because the plan and credits are organization-level, every site's Billing and Usage tabs
show the same subscription.

Core docs infrastructure — Git sync, custom domains, reader authentication, the web
editor, search, analytics, the API playground, and the MCP server — is included on
**every** plan, Free onward. Paid plans add the AI surfaces (assistant, writing agent,
workflows), SSO and role-based permissions from **Team**, and advanced insights +
multi-repo from **Pro**. Enterprise holds the procurement items: SCIM, security and
legal review, SLAs, migration, and dedicated support.

## The 30-day trial

Every new account starts with **30 days of everything** — Pro-level features plus a
one-time grant of **5,000 AI credits**, no card required. When the trial ends, the
organization lands on Free: docs keep rendering exactly as before, and AI features
pause until a plan is chosen. Settings → Billing shows the countdown, and the trial-gated
features carry a **Trialing** badge in the dashboard sidebar.

## AI credits

Credits are the accounting unit for AI usage. Every assistant answer, writing-agent
run, and workflow run meters the model tokens it actually used and converts them to
credits with a published rate table — a typical assistant answer costs around **10
credits**. Settings → Usage shows credits remaining, the **reset date**, and a table of
recent operations with their token count and credit cost.

Credits come from three buckets, consumed in this order:

1. **Trial** — the one-time signup grant (expires with the trial).
2. **Monthly** — the plan's included pool, refreshed each billing period (the "Next
   reset" date on the Usage page). Unused monthly credits don't roll over.
3. **Packs** — one-time top-ups purchased on any paid plan. Packs never expire.

## Hard caps and overage

By default a plan is **hard-capped**: when the credit pool runs out, AI features pause
until the next period (or a pack purchase) — there are no surprise bills. Owners and
admins can opt in to **overage** on the Usage page, which lets usage continue past
the included pool at the plan's per-credit overage rate (Team $0.008, Pro $0.006).

## Managing the subscription

Upgrades go through Stripe Checkout from Settings → Billing (pack purchases from
Settings → Usage). Once an organization is on a paid plan, choosing a different plan or
interval switches the existing subscription in place, with proration — no second
subscription is created.

**Downgrading to Free** is on the current-plan card: it cancels at the end of the
billing period (with a confirm step), and can be resumed any time before then. Docs
keep rendering on Free; AI features stop when the plan lapses. Card changes and
invoices are self-serve through the Stripe customer portal ("Manage billing").

Only organization **owners and admins** can change billing; members can view the page.

Self-hosting? The renderer and control plane are open source — a self-hosted
deployment has no credit meter and brings its own model keys.
