Your site tells search engines what it has, without you maintaining a list. Two files are
generated from the same navigation your sidebar is built from, and served on your own domain.

## sitemap.xml

`https://your-site/sitemap.xml` lists every page a search engine can actually fetch, in
navigation order, as absolute URLs on the host that served it. A custom domain lists itself
rather than the subdomain behind it.

Three things are left out, and each for a reason:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pages behind reader authentication">
    A crawler has no reader session, so the sitemap describes what an anonymous visitor can
    retrieve. On a site with [reader access](/auth/reader-auth) enabled, that is the public
    subset. Listing a gated page would publish its URL and send crawlers to a page they cannot
    open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pages marked noindex">
    `noindex: true` in a page's frontmatter takes it out of the sitemap, out of
    [llms.txt](/features/llms-txt), and out of search engines. One switch, every surface.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="External links in your navigation">
    A nav entry pointing at another site is somebody else's URL. It stays in your sidebar and
    out of your sitemap.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

By default the sitemap lists pages a reader can reach from the navigation. If you keep pages
that are deliberately not in the sidebar and still want them indexed, set `seo.indexing` to
`all` in `docs.json` — the same switch that controls what `llms.txt` publishes.

```json docs.json
{
  "seo": {
    "indexing": "all"
  }
}
```

## robots.txt

`https://your-site/robots.txt` allows crawling and points at your sitemap:

```txt
User-Agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://your-site/sitemap.xml
```

It never mentions Papervine's own pages or sitemap. Your domain describes your site and
nothing else.

## Adding your site to Google Search Console

Search engines find your pages by following links, so a sitemap is an accelerator rather
than a requirement. To use it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your site as a property">
    Use the exact host readers visit, including the subdomain. A custom domain and the
    Papervine subdomain are separate properties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the sitemap">
    Enter `sitemap.xml` under Sitemaps. Google fetches it on its own schedule after that.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Self-hosting? Both files work there too, generated from the repo you serve. Nothing to
  configure.
</Note>

## What else is published

Search engines are one audience. Every site also publishes machine-readable indexes for AI
clients: [llms.txt](/features/llms-txt) for the page list and the whole corpus, a
Markdown twin of every page, and [skill.md](/features/skill-md) describing what agents can do
with your product.
