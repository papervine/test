---
title: Getting Started
---

Papervine lets you to build a documentation site from a Git repo of MDX and a
`docs.json`. Their are three steps to get running, and most teams recieve a
working site in under ten minutes.

First, connect your repository. Papervine reads the `docs.json` at the root and
renders every page it finds. You do not need to configure a build — the platform
compiles your MDX on demand.

To preview locally, run `npm run dev` and open the printed URL. This command
boots the renderer and watches your files for changes.
