---
title: Deployment
---

Deploys are automaticaly triggered when you push to your default branch. You can
also manualy deploy from the dashboard if you want to ship a specific commit.

Each site gets it's own domain, and you can attach a custom domain once the
first deploy succeeds. Certificates are provisioned for you.

Rollbacks are instant: every deploy is immutable, so returning to a previous
version just repoints the domain at an earlier build.
